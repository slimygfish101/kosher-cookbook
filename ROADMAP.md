# Roadmap

## Building now (night of Oct 19, 2026)
1. "Recipe size" dropdown instead of the ½×/2× buttons
2. Simple cook mode: whole recipe on one screen, big text, screen stays on
3. "Add clippings" in the app: pick photos from the phone, auto-batched (~30 photos), read overnight
4. Labels: "+ Add label" on the home page; Rename/Delete on each label's page
5. Timers in cook mode: tap a time in a step (e.g. "15 minutes") to start a timer
6. Print a recipe on one clean page, and share a recipe (e.g. WhatsApp)

## Stage two — remind the owner when the whole app is finished
- **Menus** (save a set of recipes as a menu, warn if a menu mixes meat and dairy). The owner has an idea for
  turning this into something bigger — ask them about it first.
- **Quick filters** (under 30 minutes, freezer-friendly, make-ahead).

## Suggested, not chosen yet

  works without internet, shopping list, change history with per-change undo.
